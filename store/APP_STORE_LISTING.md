## Description (EN)
A daily companion for the Christian life. Every morning a fresh devotional: a Scripture passage, a serious meditation drawn from Bonhoeffer, C.S. Lewis, Watchman Nee, Bunyan, Keller and Grudem, pointed questions for reflection, a closing prayer and one concrete call to walk differently today.

• 365 daily devotionals, one for every date of the year
• 12 study topics with 84 lessons: identity, the cross, discipleship, apologetics, prayer, suffering, the Holy Spirit, sin and grace, the church, eternity, the Sabbath, the holiness of God
• Calendar and archive to revisit any day
• English and Spanish (Reina-Valera 1909 for the Scripture text), chosen once on the start page
• Community blog, sermons and music from the congregation

Free, no account needed. Your reading preferences stay on your device.
